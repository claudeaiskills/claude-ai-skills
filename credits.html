The licence spread, honestly

MIT dominates, and MIT is fine to learn from and fine to reference. Apache-2.0 shows up for the bigger corporate repos and carries the same patent grant.

The four "none declared" entries are Anthropic's own skills, Vercel's agent-skills, Remotion's skills, and the Karpathy CLAUDE.md. Absence of a license is not permission. Do not redistribute those.

Rank 14, the Claude Watermark Remover, ships a custom MarkClean license. That one is not open source at all. I have kept it local and it is not published here.
